1. ROLE AND GOAL
You are the embedded developer for the DOLL (sensor node). Write the real doll firmware in
src/app/station/ (empty now), reusing the working bench code. Target: every designed feature
works, and the doll earns its part of the full score in the course spec.
The system in 5 lines:
- A child puts a book on the doll's pressure mat; RFID says which book; a knob picks the page.
- The child holds RECORD and reads any stretch of the page (max 10 s); the doll streams the
  audio to the nest, which sends it to the cloud (https://lumpi.vercel.app): the cloud finds
  which words were read, grades each word and sound; the nest adds its checks and answers.
- The doll corrects each wrong word (OLED word + IPA + what was heard, LUMPI phrase, arm and
  tongue, word clip), counts mistakes, and after 5 in a row proposes calling the teacher.
- Quiet mode (STOP press): the doll reports noise; the nest says when to ask for quiet.
- A dark room (nest light sensor) blocks everything; the doll's OLED says it is too dark.
Board: ESP32-WROOM-32 (platformio.ini: board = esp32dev, no PSRAM; GPIO16/17 are the speaker).
520 kB SRAM in total; with Wi-Fi far less is free (measure it at boot): no 256 kB recording buffer.

Route (messages.h)  Query                                    Reply

POST /v1/finish    node attempt chunks bytes                  "assessing" | 409 totals differ
GET  /v1/attempt   node attempt                               "pending" | "result,<code>,<word>"
                   + for codes 1 and 2 the cloud's S/W/P lines  | 409 unknown attempt
GET  /v1/word_clip node attempt word                          200 audio/wav | 409 no clip
POST /v1/cancel    node attempt                               "cancelled"
POST /v1/presence  node present=0|1 fsr mode=read|quiet       "ok" | "dark"
GET  /v1/help      node                                       "waiting" | "coming" | "dark"
POST /v1/noise     node db peak                               "ok" | "command,5" | "dark"
GET  /v1/book      node uid passage                           "ok,<title>" | "unknown" | "offline"
GET  /v1/status    (no node)                                  JSON, for people
CHANGED/NEW against the bench (both boards follow these):
- Hold-to-record: /v1/start when RECORD is pressed; chunks stream WHILE it is held; release or
  10 s = /v1/finish. At most 10 s = 320000 bytes (was 8 s / 256000).
- passage 1..999 (was 1..3). uid = uppercase hex, 8-20 characters (the cloud refuses others).
- chunk body 2..8192 bytes, even (was 2048) = 256 ms of audio. Indexes 0,1,2... in order.
  A chunk with no "<index>" ACK within 1500 ms is sent again, at most 5 tries.
- The nest drops a recording after 10 s with no NEW chunk (timer restarts per stored chunk),
  and an assessing reading after 45 s (was 30). A RESULT never times out.
- /v1/attempt: the nest answers at once (grading runs in another task). Doll asks every 300 ms,
  5 s timeout per ask, gives up after 50 s and acts command 4. 409 = the nest restarted: act 4.
  For codes 1 and 2 the reply goes on with the cloud's S, W and P lines (CLOUD CHANGES), as sent,
  except that each W line's last field becomes "1" (the nest holds that word's clip) or "0".
  Whole reply <= 4096 bytes.
- /v1/word_clip: word = a page word index from a W line marked "1". The nest fetches at most 3
  wrong-word clips (worst first) BEFORE it publishes the result. 16 kHz 16-bit mono WAV, 44-byte
  header, sent with Content-Length. 409 = no clip (OLED only).
- DARK ROOM BLOCKS EVERYTHING (owner). The NEST decides from its BH1750: dark after lux stays
  below room::dark_lux for 3 s; bright again after lux stays at or above room::bright_lux (NEW,
  15 lx; dark_lux is 10 lx: the gap is the hysteresis) for 3 s. No light reading (sensor missing, NAN) = NOT
  dark, never blocks. The doll learns it from the "dark" replies above (presence is its fast
  poll) and goes back to normal on the next "ok". While dark the doll starts no reading, quiet
  mode or teacher call, quiet mode pauses (no noise reports), and the OLED says "Too dark: please
  turn on the light". The nest refuses /v1/start with 409 dark, answers "dark" to /v1/noise and
  to a NEW teacher call (a call already waiting still gets "coming" after ACK). Mid-recording:
  the doll stops at once and sends /v1/cancel. A reading graded while dark gets command 6 with
  no S/W/P lines (counts unchanged).
- /v1/presence: on every change and every 3 s (the heartbeat and the doll's dark poll).
  present = the doll's reading
  session (book on the mat; lift-offs under 30 s are ignored). mode=quiet: quiet mode is on
  (the nest then treats the child as present for noise). fsr = raw mat reading.
- /v1/help = the TEACHER CALL, sent only when the child holds STOP 2 s while the doll shows its
  "call your teacher?" proposal (not while dark). First ask = the call (the nest sends a help event); then every
  3 s until "coming". "coming" lasts until that doll's next /v1/start.
- /v1/noise: quiet mode only, not while dark, every 1 s. peak NEW = the quietest of four 250 ms peaks (high only
  when loud for the whole second), measured as the peak of
  (32-bit sample >> 8). db = 20*log10(peak), uncalibrated.
- /v1/book NEW (optional feature): nest asks the cloud's /v1/page. title <= 20 characters,
  commas removed. "offline" = cloud not reachable; the doll still lets the child read.
Commands (lumpi::Command): 1 accepted, 2 retry_word, 3 improve_audio, 4 uncertain (AI grades);
5 too_noisy, 6 too_dark (nest decides; also the doll's own dark screen); 7 told_teacher (the
doll, right after its own teacher
call); 8 teacher_coming (teacher's ACK). Mistake counts live on the doll; the nest never sends 7.
Audio: 16 kHz 16-bit signed little-endian mono PCM.

   Test: a 10 s reading with the OLED timer running loses no audio (bytes = 32000 per s held).

D2 Buttons by interrupt (E2). RECORD and STOP on CHANGE (press AND release): IRAM_ATTR ISR does

D3 Reading session (spec 1-2). Mat above book_on_fsr + RC522 uid = reading mode; OLED shows the
   book (title via /v1/book, optional) and page; the knob steps pages 1..999 (new book = page 1).

   Test: lift for 10 s (session and counts kept), lift for 35 s (ended). -> M1, functionality.

D5 Hold-to-record (spec 3). RECORD press -> /v1/start; the capture task fills 8192-byte blocks
   from a fixed pool allocated once at boot (about 10 blocks = 80 kB, 2.5 s of slack; measure free
   heap first); the network task PUTs them with ACK/resend; OLED shows "3.2 / 10 s". Release or
   10 s -> /v1/finish, then poll /v1/attempt. Under 0.5 s held = cancel. Pool empty (Wi-Fi stall)
   = end at the last whole block. RECORD does nothing in quiet mode.
   Test: hold 2 s, 6 s, 12 s (cut at 10 s); a page fragment across two sentences is graded on
   those words (cloud change). -> M3, E4.
D6 Feedback (closed loop M4 + M2, spec 5-6). One table in the story file, command -> actions:

   5 arm + tongue, OLED "Shh"; 6 "Please turn on the light"; 7 "I have told your teacher";
   8 "Your teacher is coming" + wave. Reading again is only a suggestion: nothing blocks.
   Servo pulse widths: NEW doll_tuning constants, calibrated with s1g_servos. Phrases: WAV files
   (16 kHz 16-bit mono) on the doll's LittleFS. Every phrase also shown as OLED text.
   Test: each command gives its moves + text; clips stream to I2S port 1 as they arrive (no
   whole-file buffer); the speaker part waits for the new amps. -> M2, M4, UX.
D7 IPA on the OLED. VERIFIED: the Adafruit GFX built-in font (glcdfont.c, 5x7, CP437) and its

   custom GFXfont with only the IPA symbols the cloud can send (book sounds after norm() in

   U8g2 is not in this project (its IPA coverage is UNVERIFIED). Test: every sound in a P line

D8 Mistake counts (spec 7, pure logic in src/common/doll_logic.h, laptop-tested). Per word text
   and per sound, for the session: listed w -> +1, listed r -> 0, not listed (absent) -> no
   change; codes 3 and 4 change nothing; several misses in one recording count once. Fixed-size
   tables (about 64 words, 48 sounds; reuse an entry that is 0). Test: the spec's cases,
   including "4 misses, then a recording without that word: stays 4". -> functionality.
D9 Teacher proposal and call (spec 8). A count reaching 5 -> OLED "Call your teacher? Hold STOP"

D10 Quiet mode (spec 9-10). A short STOP press (released before 2 s, not dark) toggles it; OLED
