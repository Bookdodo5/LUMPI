CHANGED/NEW against the bench (both boards follow these):
- Hold-to-record: /v1/start when RECORD is pressed; chunks stream WHILE it is held; release or
  10 s = /v1/finish. At most 10 s = 320000 bytes (was 8 s / 256000).
- passage 1..999 (was 1..3). uid = uppercase hex, 8-20 characters (the cloud refuses others).
- chunk body 2..8192 bytes, even (was 2048) = 256 ms of audio. Indexes 0,1,2... in order.
  A chunk with no "<index>" ACK within 1500 ms is sent again, at most 5 tries.
- The nest drops a recording after 10 s with no NEW chunk (timer restarts per stored chunk),
  and an assessing reading after 45 s (was 30). A RESULT never times out.
- /v1/attempt: the nest answers at once (grading runs in another task). Doll asks every 300 ms,
  5 s timeout per ask, gives up after 50 s and acts command 4. 409 = the nest restarted: act 4.
  For codes 1 and 2 the reply goes on with the cloud's S, W and P lines (CLOUD CHANGES), as sent,
  except that each W line's last field becomes "1" (the nest holds that word's clip) or "0".
  Whole reply <= 4096 bytes.
- /v1/word_clip: word = a page word index from a W line marked "1". The nest fetches at most 3
  wrong-word clips (worst first) BEFORE it publishes the result. 16 kHz 16-bit mono WAV, 44-byte
  header, sent with Content-Length. 409 = no clip (OLED only).
- DARK ROOM BLOCKS EVERYTHING (owner). The NEST decides from its BH1750: dark after lux stays
  below room::dark_lux for 3 s; bright again after lux stays at or above room::bright_lux (NEW,
  15 lx: the gap is the hysteresis) for 3 s. No light reading (sensor missing, NAN) = NOT
  dark, never blocks. The doll learns it from the "dark" replies above (presence is its fast
  poll) and goes back to normal on the next "ok". While dark the doll starts no reading, quiet
  mode or teacher call, quiet mode pauses (no noise reports), and the OLED says "Too dark: please
  turn on the light". The nest refuses /v1/start with 409 dark, answers "dark" to /v1/noise and
  to a NEW teacher call (a call already waiting still gets "coming" after ACK). Mid-recording:
  the doll stops at once and sends /v1/cancel. A reading graded while dark gets command 6 with
  no S/W/P lines (counts unchanged).
- /v1/presence: on every change and every 3 s (the heartbeat and the doll's dark poll).
  present = the doll's reading
  session (book on the mat; lift-offs under 30 s are ignored). mode=quiet: quiet mode is on
  (the nest then treats the child as present for noise). fsr = raw mat reading.
- /v1/help = the TEACHER CALL, sent only when the child holds STOP 2 s while the doll shows its
  "call your teacher?" proposal (not while dark). First ask = the call (the nest sends a help event); then every
  3 s until "coming". "coming" lasts until that doll's next /v1/start.
- /v1/noise: quiet mode only, not while dark, every 1 s. peak NEW = the quietest of four 250 ms peaks (high only
  when loud for the whole second), measured as the peak of
  (32-bit sample >> 8). db = 20*log10(peak), uncalibrated.
- /v1/book NEW (optional feature): nest asks the cloud's /v1/page. title <= 20 characters,
  commas removed. "offline" = cloud not reachable; the doll still lets the child read.
Commands (lumpi::Command): 1 accepted, 2 retry_word, 3 improve_audio, 4 uncertain (AI grades);
5 too_noisy, 6 too_dark (nest decides; also the doll's own dark screen); 7 told_teacher (the
doll, right after its own teacher
call); 8 teacher_coming (teacher's ACK). Mistake counts live on the doll; the nest never sends 7.
Audio: 16 kHz 16-bit signed little-endian mono PCM.

4. FEATURES (build in this order; each: acceptance test -> what it earns)
N1 Tasks and queues (E1). At least: http task (answers dolls; must never wait for the cloud),
   room task (sensors every 1 s, ACK, OLED, real-time decisions), cloud task (grading queue,
   then outbox; lowest, it blocks for seconds). Queue: "node to grade" (http -> cloud). One
   mutex around the dolls' state, never held during network or flash I/O. Reasoned priorities
   as comments. Test: while the cloud task waits 11 s, /v1/attempt still answers "pending" in
   under 200 ms. -> E1 (2 pts), architecture.
N2 Exactly two dolls at once (scalability 3 pts). Each doll's recording is appended chunk by
   chunk to its own LittleFS file (/rec/<node>.pcm; 2 x 320000 bytes + 2 x 3 clips + 64-event
   outbox in the 1.2 MB partition, about 0.75 MB: check free space at boot), sent to the
   cloud by streaming the File (HTTPClient::sendRequest(type, Stream*, size); add that overload
   to lumpi_net.h). No per-doll RAM buffer. Measure flash write speed (the proposal's risk).
   Test: two dolls record 10 s at the same moment; both graded, no mix-up (CSV). -> II, Q10.
N3 Sessions and routes (M3, scalability 1 pt): every route of the shared contract, Session per
   doll, resent chunks ACKed and not stored twice, 409 with reasons as 5_nest.cpp. Keep each
   doll's result text (<= 4096 bytes) until its next /v1/start.
N4 Real-time dark-room control (architecture "immediate control actions", M4 closed loop):
   the room task updates DarkState every 1 s (rule in the shared contract, laptop-tested); the
   http task answers "dark" / 409 dark from it; decide_command() gives 6 to a reading graded
   while dark (no S/W/P lines). OLED shows "DARK". Test: as the doll's D11b, plus light sensor
   unplugged -> nothing blocked, CSV dark lines show each change.
N5 Word clips: for code 2, fetch up to 3 wrong words' clips (worst first, addresses from the
   W lines) into /clip/<node>-<index>.wav BEFORE publishing the result (3 s each; a failure
   marks that W line "0"); serve them with a binary file reply (tiny_http::reply is text only).
N6 Teacher call + ACK (E2, UX). /v1/help first ask = call (not while dark): HelpState stuck,
   help event, OLED.

   every waiting doll (HelpState::ack). Test: calls from 2 dolls, one press, both get "coming";
   CSV ack shows the wait. -> E2, UX, M6 trigger.
N7 Quiet mode (designed feature): NoiseState per doll (quiet_study_logic.h, threshold
   NoiseState::default_threshold, 30 s cooldown); child_present = presence mode=quiet,

N8 Room and teacher screen (M1 gateway sensors, UX): lux and distance every 1 s; room event

N9 Store-and-forward (E4): every event goes to a LittleFS outbox (one file per event, max 64,
